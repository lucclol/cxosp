Market Signals & Competitive Intelligence
Key Market Signals
Q2 2026 events shaping the landscape
Biotech M&A tops $106B year-to-date — on track for the best year since before COVID; GSK–Nuvalent ($10.6B, Jun 9) and AbbVie–Apogee ($10.9B, Jun 22) follow within weeks.
Jun
IPO window reopens — Parabilis raises a record $770.5M (Jun 9) and Kardigan $460M (Jun 18); Q2 closes with 10 biotech IPOs and $3.6B, versus zero US biopharma IPOs in Q2 2025.
Jun
Licensing hits a five-year record — $83B of licensing and R&D alliances in Q2; China accounts for ~⅓ of global out-licensing value in H1 ($106B), with average China deal size up 42% QoQ.
Jun
AI drug discovery goes mainstream — Isomorphic Labs' $2.1B Series B is the largest biotech round of 2026; AI-native biotechs have raised over $6B year-to-date, about one in six biotech VC deals.
Apr
Oral GLP-1 era begins — Foundayo approved Apr 1; Kailera's $718.8M IPO (Apr) briefly the largest biotech IPO ever. Next-generation modalities draw the quarter's biggest deals: Lilly–Kelonia (in vivo CAR-T, up to $7B) and Gilead–Tubulis (ADC, up to $5B).
Apr
Sources CNBC (Jun 4, 2026); BioPharma Dive; DealForma via Endpoints; Locust Walk Q2 2026; Crunchbase News; UK BIA; company announcements.
